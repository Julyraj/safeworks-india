# Content review list (October 2026)

While finding the official documents for every regulation (version 3.3), the research turned up places where the
library's **text** may be out of date. None of these has been changed in the app yet. Check each against the
official text, then update the entry in `src/data/` and tick it here.

Status of each finding: **official** = seen on an official site; **secondary** = only reported by a secondary
source (law firm, news) and needs an official copy before changing the app.

## India – central

- [ ] **nbc-2016** – BIS withdrew the National Building Code 2016 (SP 7:2016) on 30 Apr 2026; replaced by SP 7:2026 "National Building Construction Standards". Gazette notice: egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2026/272177.pdf. *(official, from the research; re-check)*
- [ ] **boilers-2025, ka-boiler-rules-1982, tn-boiler-rules** – Indian Boiler Regulations 2026 reportedly published 1 Oct 2026 (in force 6 Oct 2026), replacing IBR 1950; Boilers Act 2025 in force since 1 May 2025, repealing the 1923 Act. *(secondary for IBR 2026; no official PDF found yet)*
- [ ] **bis-ppe, bis-ppe-2** – IS 15298 (Part 2):2016 safety footwear withdrawn, replaced by IS 15298 (Part 2):2024; IS 2925 helmets under compulsory ISI certification since 23 Apr 2024; IS 15322 is particle filters, gas/combined filters are IS 15323. *(official, BIS pages)*
- [ ] **water-codes** – IS 13416 (Part 5) is "Fire protection", not drowning prevention; the Merchant Shipping Act 1958 is replaced by the Merchant Shipping Act 2025. *(official)*
- [ ] **air-water** – Water (Amendment) Act 2024 (Act 5 of 2024) decriminalised Water Act offences (applies where adopted — check Karnataka and Tamil Nadu); the Jan Vishwas Act 2023 amended the Air Act. *(official Gazette)*
- [ ] **gas-cylinders** – hydrogen amendment is 2024 (not 2025); three 2026 amendments incl. G.S.R. 315(E) and 447(E) (rule 48 dropped; denser stacking in certified racks); SMPV(U) licence forms are LS-1A/1B/1C (storage) and LS-2/2A/2B (transport), not "Form III". *(official, PESO / Gazette)*
- [ ] **explosives** – licence forms run LE-1 to LE-11 (LE-7 transport, LE-10 shot-firer, LE-11 foreman); amendments in 2024, 2025 and 2026. *(official, PESO)*
- [ ] **petroleum** – Class B: 2,500 L licence exemption only when no container exceeds 1,000 L (larger = bulk); Class C exempt up to 45,000 L in bulk — the diesel "> 2,500 L" point may mislead. *(official, PESO guide)*
- [ ] **eia-2006** – item 8(a) covers 20,000 to < 1,50,000 m² built-up area and excludes industrial sheds, schools, colleges and hostels. *(official, consolidated notification)*
- [ ] **electricity, ht-codes** – CEA (Safety and Electric Supply) Amendment Regulations 2026 exist. *(official, CEA)*
- [ ] **child-labour** – India Code's PDF is the pre-2016 text; the app links the Tamil Nadu Labour Department's amended text instead. *(official)*
- [ ] **ir-code / osh-central-rules** – Industrial Relations (Central) Rules 2026 G.S.R. 342(E) and OSH (Central) Rules 2026 G.S.R. 345(E), 8 May 2026: add direct eGazette PDFs by hand (eGazette blocks automated access).
- [ ] **ecsbc** – the Ministry of Power star-rating notice (≥ 20,000 m²) is still a draft (June 2026). *(official)*
- [ ] **ccts** – GHG Emission Intensity Target Rules 2025 (G.S.R. 739(E), 8 Oct 2025) now linked. *(official)*
- [ ] **mines** – DGMS and India Code still list the Mines Act and its regulations; no mines rules under the OSH Code yet. *(official)*

## Tamil Nadu

- [ ] **tn-fire** – Tamil Nadu Fire and Rescue Services Act 2025 repeals the 1985 Act (amended by TN Act 16 of 2026); buildings in categories A (fire licence), B (fire safety certificate, 3 years), C (self-certification every 3 years); commencement date to confirm. The "annual renewal / NOC" wording needs review. *(official Gazette Bill; commencement unconfirmed)*
- [ ] **tn-lifts** – Act amended 2025, Rules amended Feb 2026 (G.O.Ms.No.13, Energy): online applications, permission valid 6 months, licence 5 years, new penalties and compounding, e-Forms 1–34. *(official)*
- [ ] **tn-groundwater** – the TN Groundwater (Development and Management) Act 2003 was repealed in 2013 (in force 14 Sep 2013). *(official, India Code repeal Act)*
- [ ] **tn-factories-amend-2023** – the 2023 Bill (12-hour flexible working) was put on hold and withdrawn in May 2023. *(secondary)*
- [ ] **tn-cimah** – full text of the 1994 Rules not online officially; DISH checklist PDF linked. *(official)*

## Karnataka

Karnataka government sites refuse automated access, so several state rules still link to the Labour Department homepage.
Open labour.karnataka.gov.in / dpal.karnataka.gov.in from India and send the PDF links to add them.

- [ ] **ka-fire** – Fire NOC threshold is 21 m and above for high-rise buildings (Fire Force (Amendment) Act 2023, from 24 Mar 2023), not 15 m; a further amendment about Sept 2025. *(secondary)*
- [ ] **ka-shops** – 2026 amendment (assent 3 Sep, notified 4 Sep 2026) changes section 25 provisions on women's night work and transport; LA Bill No. 23 of 2026 linked. *(official Bill; final text to confirm)*
- [ ] **ka-building** – BBMP replaced by the Greater Bengaluru Authority and five city corporations (Sept 2025); Karnataka Municipal Corporations Model Building (Amendment) Bye-laws 2026 and a draft second amendment. *(secondary)*
- [ ] **ka-lifts** – amendment notified about Sept 2025. *(secondary)*
- [ ] **ka-safety-audit** – notification LD 75 KBN 2024 dated 1 Feb 2025. *(secondary)*
- [ ] **ka-mah-rules** – correct title is probably "Control of Industrial Major Accident Hazards (Karnataka) Rules, 1994". *(secondary)*
- [ ] **ka-factories-amend-2023, ka-bocw-rules** – the OSH Code repealed the Factories Act 1948 and BOCW Act 1996 from 21 Nov 2025; confirm what survives of the state amendments and rules. *(inference)*
- [ ] **ka-osh-rules** – final Karnataka OSH rules not yet notified; still a draft. *(official, absence)*
- [ ] **ka-lifts / emergency contacts** – `ceig.karnataka.gov.in` no longer exists (site name not found in the weekly link check). The card's official link now points to India Code; find the Electrical Inspectorate's current website for the emergency contacts list. *(official, link check)*
